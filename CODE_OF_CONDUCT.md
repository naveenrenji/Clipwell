# Code of Conduct

Clipwell is for everyone who wants a better clipboard. To keep this a welcoming place to work on it:

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Disagree with ideas, not people.
- **Keep it constructive.** Give specific, actionable feedback. Say what you tried, what happened and what you expected.
- **Be patient.** Maintainers and contributors are volunteers, and many people here are learning.
- **No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention** of any kind, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's personal information, and never paste real clipboard history or secrets into an issue. Redact before sharing.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block participants who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code of conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
